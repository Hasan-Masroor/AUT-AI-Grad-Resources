# AUT – Graduate AI Course Resources

This repository provides a collection of handwritten lecture notes and past exam questions for graduate-level courses at **Amirkabir University of Technology (AUT)**, with a primary focus on Artificial Intelligence and related courses.

The available materials vary by course and may include handwritten notes, past examination papers, or both, offering students additional resources for studying and exam preparation.
